[[glossary]]
= Glossary

[glossary]
AES type::
The type of advanced electronic signature (or seal) carried by a business document. The allowed types are set with `connector.business-document.allowed-advanced-system-types`.

AS4::
The OASIS ebMS 3.0 profile used by the e-CODEX gateway to exchange messages between access points.

ASiC-S::
The signed container format (Associated Signature Container, simple) in which the Connector packages the business document, its attachments and the trust-ok token for transport.

Backend::
The national application that submits business messages to the Connector and receives messages from it. The Connector knows each backend as a *link partner* of type `BACKEND`.

Business domain::
A named lane of the Connector that groups a P-Mode and its services, actions and parties. The default business domain is `default_business_domain`.

Business message::
A message that carries a business document and optional attachments between two parties.

Conversation identifier::
The AS4 identifier that groups related messages. The routing uses it to deliver replies to the backend that started the conversation.

DSS::
The Digital Signature Service library of the European Commission. The Connector uses it to build and validate signatures and to read trusted lists.

ebMS identifier::
The ebMS message identifier of a message. For outbound messages the Connector generates it unless the backend sets it. Evidences refer to the business message by this identifier.

Evidence::
A signed receipt about a step in the life of a business message, based on the ETSI REM standard: for example `SUBMISSION_ACCEPTANCE`, `RELAY_REMMD_ACCEPTANCE`, `DELIVERY` or `RETRIEVAL`, and their negative counterparts.

Gateway::
The e-CODEX access point (Domibus) that exchanges AS4 messages with the gateways of other participants. The Connector reaches it through the JMS queues `connector.queues.gateway-*`. It is the built-in *link partner* `default_gateway`, of type `GATEWAY`.

Link partner::
A system the Connector exchanges messages with: either a backend or the gateway. Backend link partners are configured under `connector.link.backend[n].*`. Two link partners are built in and not configured there: the gateway `default_gateway` and the backend `default_test_backend` used for connector-to-connector tests. `GET /api/v1/link-partners` lists them.

LOTL::
The List of Trusted Lists published by the European Commission. It points to the trusted lists of the member states.

P-Mode::
The processing mode: the XML configuration that defines which services, actions and parties are allowed and how messages are exchanged. The Connector loads the P-Mode of each business domain and uses its truststore to validate inbound containers.

REM::
Registered Electronic Mail, the ETSI standard on which the e-CODEX evidences are based. *REM-MD* is the message dispatcher; the *relay REM-MD* evidences confirm that the message was passed on.

Routing rule::
A rule that selects the backend that receives an inbound message, based on a match clause over the message properties. Rules are configured under `connector.routing.backend-rules[n].*`.

Sender mode::
The way the Connector hands over a message to a backend: `push` (the Connector calls the backend), or `pull` and `passive` (the backend downloads the message).

TL::
A Trusted List published by a member state. It lists the qualified trust service providers.

Transport step::
The record of the transport of a message to one link partner, with its number of attempts and its status history. The statuses are `SUBMITTED`, `READY_FOR_PULL`, `PULLED`, `DELIVERED` and `FAILED`.

Trust-ok token::
A token added to the ASiC-S container that states the result of the validation of the business document's signature.
